# 30-Day Marketing Playbook — airesumetailor.com

**Goal:** First 50–100 paying customers ($500–$1,900+) in 30 days with $0 ad spend.  
**Core Hook:** *"Stop getting auto-billed $39/mo while you're unemployed. Tailor your resume to any job description in 15 seconds — $9 one-time pass, zero subscriptions."*

---

## Week 1: Proof Assets + Community Seeding (Days 1–7)

### 1. Record 3 Before/After Screen-Capture Videos (30–45 sec each)
Use real anonymized resumes from `evals/golden-dataset.json` (e.g., Admin Assistant, Warehouse Supervisor, Software Engineer):
- **Hook (0–3s):** *"Why your resume gets ghosted in 6 seconds (and how to fix it without paying $39/mo)."*
- **Middle (3–25s):** Paste a messy resume + real job posting into `airesumetailor.com`. Show the before/after diff, missing ATS keywords, and clean PDF export.
- **CTA (25–35s):** *"First tailor is free, passes start at $9 one-time—no subscription trap. Link in bio."*
- Post daily to **TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts**.

### 2. Daily Helpful Reddit Comments (20 min/day)
Target: `r/resumes`, `r/jobs`, `r/layoffs`, `r/careerguidance`, `r/recruitinghell`.
- **Rule:** Never spam links. Give 3 genuine, specific bullet rewrites to someone asking for resume feedback, then add at the bottom: *"I ran this through airesumetailor.com (first one's free, no subscription) to catch the missing keywords from the JD."*
- Aim for **5 genuinely helpful comments/day** = 150 comments in 30 days.

---

## Week 2: LinkedIn + Niche Job-Seeker Groups (Days 8–14)

### 3. LinkedIn "Tear-Down" Posts (3x/week)
- Post a side-by-side carousel: *"Before (ignored by ATS) vs. After (11 targeted fixes in 15 seconds)"*.
- End with: *"Comment 'RESUME' and I'll send you the free link (no credit card, no subscription)."*

### 4. Facebook & Discord Job-Search Groups
- Search for local/industry job-seeker groups (Healthcare, Admin, Warehouse/Logistics, Tech Layoffs).
- Share a short post offering to tailor 5 people's resumes for free in the comments—then link them to `airesumetailor.com`.

---

## Week 3: High-Intent Search + Partnerships (Days 15–21)

### 5. "Alternative To" SEO & Forum Replies
You already have `/alternatives/zety`, `/alternatives/jobscan`, `/alternatives/teal`, `/alternatives/rezi`, `/alternatives/resume-io`.
- Search Reddit & Quora for `"Zety charged me"`, `"Resume.io cancel"`, `"Jobscan too expensive"`.
- Reply empathetically and mention `airesumetailor.com` as a **$9 one-time pass with zero auto-renewal**.

### 6. Career Coaches & University Career Centers (10 emails/day)
- Offer career coaches a free 30-day pass to test with their clients.

---

## Week 4: Double Down on What Converts (Days 22–30)

- Check Umami analytics + Stripe dashboard: identify which channel drove the most signups and paid passes.
- Cut the lowest-performing channel and 3x the top-performing one (usually TikTok/Reels or Reddit `r/resumes`).
- Email every free user who tailored 1 resume with a 1-line check-in: *"How did the application go? Need to tailor for more roles this week? Grab a $9 Sprint Pass—no subscription."*
